# Goated VIP Bonus Icons — Step 1: Style Analysis & Concept Proposals

## 1. Goated art style — what we're matching

Distilled from the 64 reference assets in `Goated_Art.zip` (chests, coins, chips, calendars,
cash, gift box, envelopes, slot machine, raffle/lottery tickets, gems, dice, roulette).

| Trait | What it looks like in the references |
|---|---|
| **Rendering** | Hand-painted, stylized 2.5D "premium mobile game" asset. Cel-shaded value blocks with soft gradient blending on top — not glossy 3D, not flat vector. |
| **Linework** | Bold, dark ink outline (deep brown on warm objects, deep navy on cool ones), varying weight — thicker on the silhouette, thinner on interior details. Comic-book edge. |
| **Form** | Chunky, slightly exaggerated proportions. Beveled edges, thick rims, heavy hardware (steel bands, rivets, brass locks). |
| **Camera** | Single hero object, 3/4 view, slightly tilted or dynamically angled. Reads instantly at small sizes. |
| **Texture** | Subtle painterly surface detail: wood grain, burlap weave, paper edges, worn steel, embossed metal. Never noisy. |
| **Light** | Warm top-left key light, crisp specular highlights on metal and coins, and a warm golden inner glow where treasure is exposed (open chests). |
| **Palette** | Rich gold/amber coins (`#F6C53A → #D9771A`), warm red-brown wood, cool steel grey bands, cash green (`#3FAE4F`), deep slate navy for "premium" objects (gift box, envelope, roulette, slot machine), with **Goated neon lime (`#D4FF00`)** as the brand accent. |
| **Brand mark** | The Goated **ram-horn glyph** (two angular horn shapes) embossed on coins, chips, wax seals and the slot-machine sign. |

**How this differs from the current bonus icons:** today's icons are generic, glossy, stock-style 3D
(e.g. the green plastic Lossback arrow, the chrome-lime Reload ring). They don't share the
ink-outlined, hand-painted language of Goated's own art, and they carry almost no brand marks.
The new set brings every bonus into the house style and puts the ram-horn glyph on the coins, chips and seals.

**A system-level idea:** the time-based bonuses should *escalate visually*:
Daily (small) → Weekly (medium) → Monthly (large/premium). Players should be able to tell which reward
is "bigger" from the art alone.

## 2. Concept proposals: three per bonus type

Each concept below becomes one of the (up to) 3 basic variants in Step 2.

### Daily Bonus  *(smallest, most frequent reward)*
- **A: Daily Coin Pouch.** A small, cinched burlap pouch in the style of Goated's money bag, stamped with the ram-horn glyph and a few Goated coins spilling out. It's the on-brand version of the current icon, sized down to sit at the bottom of the reward ladder.
- **B: Tear-off Day Calendar.** A compact version of Goated's tear-off desk calendar (red header, silver rings) with today's page peeling back to reveal a Goated coin. A lime tab marks today.
- **C: Morning Mug of Coins.** A chunky ceramic mug (slate navy, lime rim) overflowing with Goated coins instead of coffee, with a curl of golden "steam" sparkles. It says "your daily ritual".

### Weekly Bonus  *(mid-tier)*
- **A: Classic Goated Treasure Chest.** Goated's own wood-and-steel chest (Chest A), lid open, heaped with gold coins and a warm golden glow. It's the direct brand match.
- **B: Week Calendar Strip.** Goated's spiral calendar with one full lime-highlighted week row and a stack of coins in front. This echoes the existing "THU" calendar asset.
- **C: Goated Gift Box.** The slate-navy gift box with a gold ribbon, lid popping off, with coins and lime light bursting out.

### Monthly Bonus  *(largest time-based reward, premium)*
- **A: Royal Vault Chest.** A bigger, more ornate chest with dark slate wood, gold trim and a ram-horn medallion, overflowing with coins **plus** blue, gold and green gems in Goated's gem style.
- **B: Big Month Calendar + Wax Seal.** A large tear-off calendar with a gold wax seal (ram-horn emboss) and ribbon stamped on the page, with coins stacked at the base.
- **C: Bank Vault Door.** A heavy round steel vault door cracked open, with golden light and coins pouring out. The message is "the big one".

### VIP Bonus
- **A: Goated Crown.** A hand-painted gold crown with chunky ink outlines, red and blue gems in Goated's gem style, and a central lime gem.
- **B: VIP Invitation Envelope.** Goated's slate-navy envelope sealed with a lime ram-horn wax seal, with a gold card peeking out of the flap.
- **C: Crowned VIP Chip.** Goated's black-and-lime casino chip (Chip B), standing on edge with a small gold crown perched on top.

### Instant Rakeback
- **A: Goated Rake.** Keeps the current pun but redraws it in style: a wooden handle with a lime rake head, raking in a small pile of Goated coins.
- **B: Croupier's Table Rake.** The real casino croupier stick (the origin of "rake"), sweeping a small pile of Goated chips (red and lime).
- **C: Coin Magnet.** A classic red-and-steel horseshoe magnet with Goated coins flying back into it. It reads as "money instantly coming back".

### Lossback
- **A: Return Arrow + Coins.** A chunky curved lime arrow looping back around a stack of Goated coins. It's the on-brand evolution of the current icon.
- **B: Boomerang.** A wooden boomerang with lime stripes, mid-return, trailing a few coins. It reads as "what you lose comes back".
- **C: Life Ring.** A slate-navy and lime life preserver ring with Goated coins nested inside. It reads as "we've got your back".

### Reload
- **A: Lime Refresh Ring + Goated Coin.** Two chunky lime arrows circling a big embossed Goated coin. It's the on-brand evolution of the current icon.
- **B: Coin Battery.** A chunky battery with a lime charge level filling up and a Goated coin as the terminal cap, with small energy sparks.
- **C: Chip Rack Refill.** A casino chip tray being topped up, with stacks of red and lime Goated chips dropping into the slots.

### Level Up Bonus
- **A: Power Bolt.** A hand-painted amber-to-orange lightning bolt with ink outline, lime sparks and small gold stars. It's the on-brand evolution of the current icon.
- **B: Rank Shield.** A steel-and-gold heraldic shield with stacked lime up-chevrons and a gold star on top.
- **C: Level-Up Potion.** A round glass flask of glowing, bubbling lime potion with a cork and gold ram-horn label. It's the classic game "level up" trope.

### Tier Up Bonus
- **A: Big Lime Arrow + Coin Stacks.** Goated's lime 3D arrow (Banner_03_b style) bursting upward out of a pile of Goated coins, with gold stars.
- **B: Coin Staircase.** Three coin stacks of increasing height forming steps, with a lime arrow climbing them. It reads as "move up a tier".
- **C: Goated Rocket.** A chunky slate-navy rocket with lime fins and a ram-horn porthole, launching off a pile of coins with a golden flame.
