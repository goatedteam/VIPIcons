## Notes for the finals

- **Daily 2 (Coffer)** is close to the Weekly chest. If the time-based bonuses should read as a small → medium → large ladder, Daily 1 or 3 keeps them more distinct.
- **Tier Up 1 and 3** rendered the Gold emblem as a square slab rather than Goated's rhombus. The final prompt should state "Gold = rhombus standing on its point".
- The Goated VIP tier icons (`reference/vip_tiers/`) are only 80 px, so the model paints them in the house style rather than copying them pixel for pixel. Shapes and colours are held by the prompt.
